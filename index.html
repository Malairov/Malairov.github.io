Operating approach

Delivery starts with the business outcome, not the tool.

I define the target state, decision rights, workstreams, risks, dependencies, acceptance evidence, and handover conditions first. Tools support that delivery logic; they do not substitute for it.

  1. FrameBusiness outcome, scope, stakeholders, constraints, and acceptance.
  2. StructureWorkstreams, milestones, owners, dependencies, risks, and decision rights.
  3. ExecuteCoordinate multidisciplinary delivery, actions, changes, issues, and suppliers.
  4. ValidateTest against requirements, acceptance criteria, readiness, and live conditions.
  5. StabilizeResolve exceptions, drive adoption, close gaps, and prepare operational handover.
  6. ImproveUse performance evidence, root cause, and lessons learned to strengthen the system.
