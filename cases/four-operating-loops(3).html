Case 01 · Business transformation · Aerospace manufacturing

From Prepaid Supply to a Controlled Consignment Operating Model

Led the site-level implementation system behind an 18-month business-model transformation: requirements, workflow design, hardware vending infrastructure, data and analytics, cross-functional communication, testing, acceptance, stabilization, and transition into continuous improvement — while live production support continued.

01 / Business challenge

A commercial model change only works when every operational dependency changes with it.

The business objective was not to optimize one replenishment task. It was to make a different commercial model executable at site level without interrupting support to manufacturing.

The new model changed ownership, point-of-use behaviour, receiving, user attribution, workflow responsibilities, reporting, exception handling, and the information needed for decisions. My role was to turn those interdependent requirements into a working delivery system and keep the transition controlled until the new process could operate reliably.

Legacy state

Processes existed, but they belonged to the prepaid model.

  • Fragmented data and paper-based references
  • Manual, person-dependent planning and follow-up
  • Limited ownership-state visibility
  • Small point-of-use footprint
  • Communication and exceptions distributed across functions
Target state

A controlled operating system for consignment.

  • Structured workflows and ownership states
  • Expanded hardware point-of-use infrastructure
  • Central data and analytical controls
  • Traceable communication, issue, and escalation paths
  • Acceptance-based handoff into continuous improvement
02 / Transformation process

Seven connected delivery processes moved the account from commercial decision to stable operation.

The transformation was managed as an integrated sequence rather than a single launch. Each process created the conditions required for the next one.

01 · Discover

Requirements & current state

Interview stakeholders, map pain points, define ownership boundaries, identify risks, and translate business requirements into deliverables.

Output: scope, workstreams, acceptance logic
02 · Design

Future-state workflows

Design the process logic connecting physical stock, users, vending, receiving, procurement, billing, communication, and reporting.

Output: target operating model
03 · Build

Infrastructure & controls

Implement hardware vending expansion, digital item/location structure, reporting, issue controls, and analytical decision tools.

Output: executable business processes
04 · Validate

Test against acceptance

Use test scripts, stakeholder review, short feedback cycles, exception checks, and acceptance criteria before retiring legacy methods.

Output: accepted process state
05 · Deploy

Controlled transition

Move workflows into live use while monitoring prepaid-to-consignment ownership states, dependencies, communications, and service continuity.

Output: live target process
06 · Stabilize

Exceptions & adoption

Track failures, ownership, escalations, timing, recurring patterns, and user behaviour until the process becomes predictable.

Output: operational readiness
07 · Improve

Continuous improvement

Move accepted processes into recurring review, measure performance, and redesign controls where evidence shows recurring friction or risk.

Output: controlled improvement backlog
03 / Delivery governance

The process became manageable when every exception had an owner, state, and decision path.

01

Milestones over activity

Tracked delivery through accepted sub-projects and implementation milestones rather than counting operational tasks.

02

Risk before escalation

Risks were assessed by probability and impact, assigned owners, mitigated where the long-term value justified action, and escalated when thresholds were crossed.

03

Acceptance before handoff

Department representatives validated processes against acceptance criteria before work moved into the continuous-improvement stage.

04

One controlled data view

Preferred controlled source data and governed views over fragile multi-owner spreadsheets that could break silently and increase communication load.

ScopeWorkstreams, requirements, deliverables, acceptance
ExecutionKanban, actions, dependencies, testing
ControlRisks, issues, changes, escalation
HandoverStabilization, acceptance, CIM review
